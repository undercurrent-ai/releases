Harbor

Every listening port on your machine, and a button to kill it

"Port 3000 is already in use" — by what, started from where, and how long ago? Harbor answers that as a card per listening port: the port, a guess at the service (Vite dev server, PostgreSQL), the process, its uptime, and the project folder it was launched from.

Two clicks to kill, or to force it. A Free it box takes a port number and clears whatever is holding it. A Projects view groups ports by repository root, so you can see that three of them belong to the same checkout. A Log records ports appearing, disappearing and being signalled.

It rescans only while the window is in front. There is no daemon and nothing runs when the app is closed.

A process that ignores a polite shutdown is reported rather than assumed dead — Harbor rescans after signalling and tells you it ignored SIGTERM instead of pretending it worked.
